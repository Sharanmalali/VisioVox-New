# Participant information sheet

**Study:** Using a tool that separates the voices in a recorded conversation

**Run by:** the VisioVox capstone team, Dept. of Information Science and
Engineering, BMS College of Engineering. The supervisor is Harini S
(harinis.ise@bmsce.ac.in).

## What is this about?

We have built a website, VisioVox, that takes a video of several people talking
and separates each person's voice. You can then listen to one person, read a
transcript, and jump around the recording. We want to find out whether it
actually helps people, compared with an ordinary video player.

## What will I do?

You will watch parts of two short recorded conversations, one with VisioVox and
one with a normal video player, and answer three questions about each, such as
"what did this person say?" or "when was this mentioned?". Then you will fill in
a 10-question form about the website. The whole session takes about 25 minutes.

We are testing the **website, not you**. There are no right or wrong ways to
use it, and it is fine not to know an answer.

## What do you record?

- How long each question takes you, what you type as your answers, and your
  ticks on the form.
- A code such as P07 instead of your name.
- **We do not film you, record your voice, or record your screen.**
- This signed form is the only place your name appears, and it is kept
  separately from the results.

## How will the results be used?

They will be used in our project report and an academic paper, as numbers
across all participants (for example, "people were 30 % faster"). No one will be
identifiable. With your permission we may quote a comment you write, without
your name.

## Do I have to take part?

No. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. If you want your answers
removed later, tell the facilitator your code before
\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ (date) and we will delete them.

There are no known risks beyond those of using a laptop with headphones for 25
minutes. Please tell us if the volume is uncomfortable.
